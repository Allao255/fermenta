* screamo.asc - estagio de clipping do Tube Screamer, desenhado no LTspice 24.
* Saida no no N002 (saida do OA1). Drive normal (x=1 = maxima distorcao).
* Carregue este arquivo na GUI do Fermenta (saida = N002).
X§OA1 N003 N005 N002 IdealOpamp
R1 0 N005 470k
C1 N005 N006 0.05µ
C2 N002 N003 47p
R2 N001 N003 47k
R3 N003 N004 4k7
C3 N004 0 0.05µ
X§Dap1 N002 N003 ExtendedSchockleyDiodeAntiParallel params: Is=4.352n eta=1.905 Vth=25.8563m Rs=1m Rp=1Meg n=1
X§Plog1 N001 N002 N002 LogarithmicPotentiometer params: Rp=500k x=0.5
Vin N006 0 Vin
* block symbol definitions
.subckt IdealOpamp 1 2 3
B1 5 0 V=V(1,2)
B2 3 0 V=V(3,5)
.ends IdealOpamp
.subckt ExtendedSchockleyDiodeAntiParallel 1 2
B 1 2 I=2*Is*sinh((V(1,2)-n*Rs*I(B))/(n*eta*Vth))+(V(1,2)-n*Rs*I(B))/(n*Rp)
.ends ExtendedSchockleyDiodeAntiParallel
.subckt LogarithmicPotentiometer 1 2 3
Ra 1 2 R=Rp*(a*pow(b,x)+c)+tol
Rb 2 3 R=Rp*(1-a*pow(b,x)-c)+tol
.param tol=1u Rm=0.1
.param b=pow(1/Rm-1,2)
.param a=1/(b-1)
.param c=-a
.ends LogarithmicPotentiometer
.backanno
.end
